📖 Quick Setup Guide for EmailJS

  1. Go to EmailJS Documentation
  2. Sign up for a free account
  3. Add an Email Service (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, etc.)
  4. Create an Email Template with variables: {{assignment_title}}, {{course_name}}, {{due_date}}, {{portal_url}}
  5. Copy your Public Key, Service ID, and Template ID here
  6. Click "Send Test Email" to verify the setup
